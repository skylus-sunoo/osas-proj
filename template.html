REMINDER TO ALL APPLICANTS

SILANG CAMPUS VALIDATION PERIOD
November 25, 2024 to March 25, 2025


Application for Admission for FIRST SEMESTER AY 2025-2026


PROCEDURE FOR SUBMISSION OF APPLICATION WITH REQUIREMENTS:


All applicants must select the date and time of validation of documents submitted online.

Only applicants who are scheduled on the particular date and time will be accommodated.                                                          STRICTLY NO-WALK INS.


Step 1: On the appointment date, applicant will present the original copies of the documentary documents at the Office of the Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) Bldg. 3, Room 312, Silang Campus.


Step 2: Once the application has been validated by the OSAS personnel, the applicant will be scheduled to take the examination (on site). The applicant must download and print the exam permit from the online admission system (after validation) and bring the exam permit on the exam day.


Step 3: Transferee applicants, second course takers, and Associate, Certificate, Vocational, or Diploma Degree Holder will only undergo College evaluation and will not take the admission exam. Result of the evaluation from the College shall determine if the applicant is qualified to proceed will the application or not.



IMPORTANT NOTE
(FOR EXAMINEES)


REMINDER AND THINGS TO BRING DURING EXAMINATION

  1. Bring Pencil no. 2
  2. Bring ID card, and alcohol/sanitizer
  3. Keep your examination permit safe and secure
  4. No permit, no examination
  5. Do not be late. Be present at the examination venue 30 minutes before the time.

NOTE:

  • Only examinees who are scheduled for admission exam shall be allowed to enter the University premises and will be directed to the designated area of the examination The will not be allowed to roam around the University.
  • No food or drinks are allowed during examination.


The list of qualified applicants for Silang Campus will be released tentatively on JULY 2025 through the CvSU Silang Office of the Student Affairs and Services' Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/osascvsusilang).


The Office of the Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) of CvSU Silang Campus would like to inform all applicants that filling-out the online application form and having a control number do not mean a sure slot for admission.


All applicants are advised to remember the control number assigned to them.
